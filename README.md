# 📊 Job Market & Salary Analytics Dashboard

### 1. Project Title

📊 Job Market & Salary Analytics Dashboard

An interactive business intelligence project developed using Tableau to analyze salary trends, job titles, experience levels, employment types, company sizes, and employee locations.

---

### 2. Short Description

The Job Market & Salary Analytics Dashboard is an interactive analytics solution designed to provide insights into job market trends and salary distributions.

The project was developed using Tableau to analyze average salaries by experience level, employment type, job title, and country. It also explores company size distribution, employee residence, and employment type patterns. Interactive visualizations and filters help users explore salary trends and compare job market data across different dimensions.

---

### 3. Tech Stack

* 📊 Tableau – Used for dashboard development, interactive reporting, and data visualization.
* 🧹 Data Analysis – Used to analyze salary distributions and job market patterns.
* 📈 Data Visualization – Used to present salary trends and employment insights.
* 🌍 Geographic Visualization – Used to explore average salaries across countries.
* 🎛️ Interactive Filters – Used for dynamic analysis by company location and job title.

---

### 4. Data Source

The dashboard uses a job market and salary dataset containing information related to:

* Job titles
* Average salaries in USD
* Experience levels
* Employment types
* Company sizes
* Company locations
* Employee residence countries

The dataset was analyzed to develop interactive visualizations and explore salary patterns across different job roles, experience levels, and locations.

### Business Problem

Job market data contains salary information across different job titles, experience levels, employment types, and countries. Analyzing these factors separately can make it difficult to compare salary trends and understand the distribution of job opportunities across locations and employment categories.

### Goal of the Dashboard

The goal of this project is to provide an interactive view of job market and salary data using Tableau, enabling users to compare average salaries, explore experience-level patterns, understand employment type distributions, and analyze geographic salary differences.

### Key Visuals

* Average Salary by Experience Level and Employment Type
* Total Companies by Size and Location
* Average Salary by Country
* Top 10 Employee Residence Countries
* Experience Level Distribution
* Employment Type Distribution
* Average Salary by Job Title and Experience Level
* Company Location Filter
* Job Title Filter

### Business Impact & Insights

The dashboard helps users:

* Compare average salaries across experience levels
* Explore salary differences across employment types
* Identify countries with higher average salaries
* Analyze employee residence distribution
* Understand company size distribution
* Compare salaries across different job titles
* Explore job market patterns by company location
* Support data-driven exploration of salary trends
